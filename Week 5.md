# Weekly Journal Entry Guide

## How to Approach Your Reflection

Each week, you'll reflect on course material using **one of three approaches**. Pick the one that connects most to what you're thinking about this week.

---

## The Three Approaches

### 🔗 The Connector
**Definition:** Link this week's material to something bigger—your project, a previous concept, real-world examples, or your own experience.

**Example Prompt:** "How does this week's reading connect to what I'm working on?"

---

### 🔧 The Troubleshooter
**Definition:** Document a challenge you faced and how you worked through it—technical, conceptual, or practical.

**Example Prompt:** "What was difficult this week, and how did I solve it?"

---

### 🎯 The Critic
**Definition:** Ask a critical question about the material. Challenge an assumption, identify a gap, or explore an implication.

**Example Prompt:** "What question or assumption from this week intrigues or concerns me?"

---

## Requirements

- **Length:** 250-300 words
- **Due:** Every Friday by 11:59 PM
- **Choose ONE approach** and delete the other two
- **Tag:** Include `#journal #weekX` at the bottom

# Week 5: Week 5

**Date:** 02/13/2026
**Phase:** Journalist
**Approach Used:** Connector

## Your Reflection

This week we started our research properly, I suspect that we will soon be crafting theories based on the topics we choose. What my theory is, I have yet to decide. But, the chapter talks a lot about theories, so maybe it will help me come to a decision. A grand theory may be something like, "AI is bad for development, because it takes people's jobs." Now, I am not sure if that is true or not, but it can easily be further defined. Simply asking "How does it take someone's job?" raises further questions on to what Generative AI really does for people. While I do not have any further examples off the top of my head, I do believe that Generative AI has a place in game development, it simply is a matter of how it is used. Studios such as Embark Studios used samples of real people that they paid money too, and trained an AI to speak voice clips for their game Arc Raiders. Larian Studios, the creators of the Divinity series and Baldur's Gate 3, have said they use generative AI to give their concept artists a generalized idea of what they are going for. So, while I do not like when a company creates an entire game using nothing but generative AI, I see AI as a tool that be be used to assist in the development cycle of a game. Once it stops destroying the planet anyways, that is the first goal they need to achieve.
